Written by the agent that translated ERI_PRO, ERI_DEA, ERI_MKI, ERI_GKE, ERI_KBE, ERI_MST, ERI_MAT, ERI_MED, ERI_TEL, ERI_TEN, ERI_TFO, ERI_DAT_A.

## Decisions to keep consistent
- Address: Eriko calls the player bare {Nm} (she never uses an honorific), "you" for あなた. In ERI_PRO only, where the Japanese has the dev placeholder 主人公, she says "Protagonist" (38 uses); everywhere else it is {Nm}.
- Player calls her "Futami"; narration says "Futami" (never "Eriko"); her full name "Futami Eriko".
- Eriko's voice: short sentences, no contractions in cold/formal lines ("I am leaving now.", "I will not see you again."), contractions allowed when she softens ("I'm fine with it anytime", "I won't move"). Never exclamation-heavy; "Heh," for クスッ, "Fufu" for フフッ (soft or teasing), "Hehe..." for クスクス.
- やるわね (sarcastic) -> "Well played" (KBE, TFO, TEL); 無理ないわね + trait -> "I can see why." + "She looked/is X, after all."
- 実験 = "experiment(s)" (her kiss experiments); 味音痴 = "taste-deaf".
- すっぽかす = "stand (someone) up"; 約束 = "promise".
- Goodbye variants: さよなら = "Goodbye.", じゃあね = "See you." / "See you, {Nm}.", またね = "See you.", おやすみ = "Good night."
- Her agreement ええ = "Yes."; いいわよ/いいわ = "All right." / "That's fine."; そうね = "Right." / "I see." / "I suppose." depending on the line.
- 校門 = "school gate", the meeting place いつもの所 = "the usual place"; 屋上 = "the rooftop"; 更衣室 = "the locker room".
- 見て見ぬふりをするわよ = "They'll pretend not to see." (repeated in ERI_PRO).
- Stammer rule used for the player: そ、そう -> "R-really?" / "R-right."; ご、ごめん -> "S-sorry".
- Dev memos and dev jokes (PRO, MAT 37/58 "That's not fair TT", GKE 25) are translated literally; 主人公/モブ ("mob") kept as dev words.
- 丘の上公園 = "Hilltop Park". 海 = "the beach". 論文 = "paper"; she is a middle-school ("junior high") prodigy; 円周率 = "pi".
- The {Nm} token at the end of a line is written after a comma ("..., {Nm}.") so that the plain text has no double space.
- Pauses {W..} inside words: put between English words; leftovers cluster at the end of the nearest phrase.
