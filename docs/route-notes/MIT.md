
## Decisions from PRO, DEA, MKI, GKE, KBE, MST, MAT, MED, TEL, TEN, TFO, DAT_A (keep consistent)
- Mitsuki: no contractions in her speech (player and others may use them). Calls the player "Master" (ご主人様) or bare "{Nm}" (-さん dropped), "you" for あなた.
- Leave-taking: 失礼します = "Excuse me." ; "Well then, excuse me." ; また = "Until next time." ; おやすみなさい = "Good night."
- 喜んで = "With pleasure." ; 分かりました = "Understood." ; ぜひ = "by all means"/"Please do." ; 楽しみにしてますね = "I am looking forward to it." ; ご一緒します = "I will go with you."
- すみません = "I am sorry." ; 困ります = "that would trouble me" / "I do not know what to do" / "I am at a loss" (by context).
- そうですね (agreement) = "Yes, indeed." ; はい alone = "Yes."
- ご主{W3}人{W3}様 splits: write "Master" whole, put the waits after it ("Master.{W3}{W3}{W3}").
- Laughs: フフッ = "Fufu"; クスッ = "Hehe" (Mitsuki); stammer ご、ご主人様 = "M-Master", し、祇条さん = "S-Shijou".
- Class "Class 2-B" for Mitsuki, "Class 2-A" for the player. Name given family-given: "Shijou Mitsuki" (waits between the parts kept).
- Player to Mitsuki: casual, bare "Shijou". Mitsuki's "{Nm}{Nn}" pair written "{Nm} {Nn}" (space; checker shows a false DOUBLE_SPACE WARN).
- Words around {Nm}: avoid "{Nm} <wait> word" patterns that give a double space; reword with a comma or apostrophe.
- 婚約者 = "fiance" (no pronoun: "my fiance"); この話 (arrangement) = "this matter"; Father/Mother capitalised when she says お父様/お母様.
- 話し相手 = "conversation partner" (the reason she talks with the player).
- 海 = "the beach" when going/at the sea, "the sea" in the quip "The sea is the best!".
- Phone opening: "I apologize for the late call. This is Shijou Mitsuki speaking. Is this the home of {Nm}?"
- Jealousy variants (KBE/TFO): 方 = "girl"/"she" for the other heroine: Library=Yuumi, Year 1 floor=Narumi, boys' gossip=Mao, soccer=Asuka, Science Lab/genius=Eriko, Discipline Committee/order=Megumu. Break-up: "I will speak to Father and have this matter called off."
- Alternate voice takes (GKE, MKI, MST, MAT, MED) share the same wording; keep identical wording for the same Japanese.
- Confession: 好き = "like" ("I-I like you!") not "love".
